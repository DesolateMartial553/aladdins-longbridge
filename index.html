Common questions

Quick answers before you order. Anything else, just ring.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 30-45 minutes depending on how busy we are.

Is there a minimum order for delivery?

No minimum, but delivery is free on orders over £15.

Can you cater for parties?

Yes, call ahead for larger party or catering orders.

Do you take card payments?

Yes, card and cash are both accepted on collection and delivery.

I have an allergy. What should I do?

Please tell us about any allergies before placing your order. Call 0121 368 8887 to discuss ingredients and preparation.
